Kavya Girls Hostel — Rules & Regulations

To maintain a comfortable and disciplined living environment, all residents must strictly adhere to the following rules:

A. Visitor Protocol

  • Timing: Female visitors must depart by 5:00 PM.
  • Male Access: Males are strictly prohibited from entering the premises.
  • Overnight Stays: No outside guests are permitted to stay overnight for any reason.
  • Study Groups: Group study with non-residents is not allowed in rooms or common areas.
  • Visitor Log: All guests must register their details, purpose of visit, and exact timings.

B. Entry & Exit Policy

  • Gate Timings: Opens at 5:45 AM and closes (Curfew) at 7:30 PM.
  • Logbook: Every entry and exit must be recorded in the register.
  • Late Arrival: Entry after 7:30 PM requires prior written permission from the Warden or a call from a guardian.
  • Early Departure: For emergency exits before 6:00 AM, log the specific reason and notify the Warden.

C. Mess Schedule

Please ensure punctuality as the kitchen will not serve latecomers under any circumstances:

  • Breakfast: 8:30 AM – 9:30 AM
  • Lunch: 2:00 PM – 3:00 PM
  • Dinner: 8:00 PM – 9:00 PM

D. Personal Cooking & Celebrations

  • Notice: Inform the cook at least one hour in advance before using kitchen facilities.
  • Ingredients: Residents must provide their own vegetables, spices, and oil.
  • Hygiene: You are responsible for cleaning all utensils and the platform immediately after use.
  • Supervision: Personal cooking is only permitted while the hostel cook is on the premises.
  • Birthdays: Notify the cook one day in advance for birthday celebrations.

E. Terrace & Safety

  • Coordination: Always inform your roommate before heading to the terrace.
  • Curfew: Access to the terrace is strictly prohibited after 10:30 PM.
  • Discipline: Violators may face formal disciplinary action.

F. General Discipline

  • Utilities: Switch off all lights and fans when leaving your room.
  • Conservation: Do not waste food or water.
  • Quiet Hours: Maintain absolute silence in the corridors after 10:00 PM.
  • Vacating Notice: Management must be informed at least one month before you plan to leave.
